About OrcaSafe

What is it?

OrcaSafe is a real-time acoustic intelligence platform that uses deep learning to detect orca vocalizations. By tracking marine mammal presence, the platform helps mitigate vessel noise and prevent harmful encounters in the Salish Sea.

The Threat of Acoustic Masking

Acoustic masking occurs when anthropogenic (human-made) noise interferes with an animal's ability to hear crucial sounds. For Southern Resident Killer Whales, loud vessel noise masks their echolocation clicks and calls, severely limiting their ability to communicate, navigate, and hunt for Chinook salmon.

Speed & Noise Reduction

Reducing speed is the most effective way to lower underwater noise. The following table outlines estimated noise reductions based on vessel speed.

Guidelines for Vessel Pilots

Maintain standard navigation routes while monitoring the application. If an orca detection occurs and a vessel enters the alert radius, reduce speed to recommended guidelines to minimize acoustic interference.
